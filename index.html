Instructions

How to use

  1. Check the Meal Time. It is picked from the phone’s clock. Tap another one to change it.
  2. Type the Blood Sugar Reading in mg/dL.
  3. If the sensor shows a trend arrow, tap the matching Arrow. Tap it again to clear it.
  4. Read the Correction Dose and the coloured message under it.
  5. For each food, type its name and its carbs in grams. A row only counts when it has both.
  6. The big box at the bottom shows the total insulin. It never goes below 0.
  7. Check the numbers against the written plan before injecting.
  8. Tap Clear when you are done.

Reminders

    Privacy

    Nothing you type is saved or sent anywhere. There are no accounts or tracking. The numbers disappear when you tap Clear or close the app. After the first visit it works without internet.
